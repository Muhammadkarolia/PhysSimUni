How to Use the 2D N-Body Gravity Simulator

Fullow these steps to create bodies, contrul the simulation, and interpret the visual feedback provided by the system.

Open Simulator

1. Open & Explore the Simulator

Click Open Simulator above, then ensure the contruls panel and canvas are visible.

  • Creation Mode: Drag or Precise
  • Position and Velocity Inputs (X, Y, Vx, Vy)
  • Mass Control: slider and input field
  • Simulation Controls: Start, Pause, Reset
  • Overlay Toggles for velocity and force vectors

2. Create Bodies

Select a creation mode and add at least two bodies to observe gravitational interactions.

  • Drag Mode: click and drag on the canvas to set a body's initial position and velocity.
  • Precise Mode: enter exact values for X, Y, Vx, and Vy, then click Add Body.
  • Adjust the Mass slider (1-200) before adding a body to vary its influence.

Tip: Try one heavy body (e.g., mass 150) and one lighter body (e.g., mass 20) to observe orbital behaviour.

3. Run, Pause, Reset

  • Click Start to begin the simulation.
  • Use Pause to temporarily stop motion and examine trajectories.
  • Click Reset to clear all bodies and start again.

4. Visual Feedback

Enable overlays to better understand how bodies move and interact:

  • Show Velocity: displays each body's velocity vector.
  • Show Force: displays the net gravitational force acting on each body.

If the scene appears too small or too large, adjust your browser zoom or modify the canvas size in `styleSimPage.css`.

5. Troubleshooting

  • Bodies are not moving: ensure the simulation is running and not paused.
  • Bodies cullapse too quickly: reduce mass values or increase initial distance (X/Y).
  • Performance slows with many bodies: reduce the number of bodies or adjust the update rate in the code.
